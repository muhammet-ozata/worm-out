Worm Out! - Privacy Policy

Puzzle Game

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Worm Out! (the "App"), developed and published by Muhammet Özata ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use our mobile application available on the Google Play Store.

Worm Out! is a free-to-play puzzle game suitable for all ages. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all our users. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Key Point: We do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email address or phone number, and the App does not require an account to play. The App does use Google services that collect data: Google AdMob for advertising (Section 5) and Google Analytics for Firebase for anonymous usage measurement (Section 5A). If you choose to back up your game progress, it is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Section 5B) — that choice is yours and the App works fully without it.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Worm Out! does not:

  • Ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number or physical address
  • Require registration or login in order to play
  • Access your device's camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files
  • Collect or process any biometric data
  • Collect precise (GPS) location
  • Include social features, chat, or user-generated content
  • Sell, rent, or trade your data

What the App does collect is described in Section 5 (advertising), Section 5A (usage analytics and crash reports) and Section 5B (optional cloud backup).

3. Locally Stored Data

The App stores a small amount of non-personal game data exclusively on your device using the device's local storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device and includes:

  • Game progress: Current level, maximum level reached per category, and star ratings earned
  • User preferences: Selected avatar icon, chosen puzzle category, sound/music toggle settings, text-to-speech voice preference, vibration preference, and locale/language selection
  • Game mode: Zen mode (relaxed play) toggle state

This data is purely functional and required for the App to remember your game progress and settings. It contains no personally identifiable information. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

4. No Payments or In-App Purchases

Worm Out! is a completely free application. We do not offer any in-app purchases, subscriptions, premium features, virtual currencies, or any other paid content. No financial information (credit card numbers, bank details, payment tokens) is ever collected or processed by the App.

5. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service operated by Google LLC. These ads help us keep the App free for all users.

5.1 Types of Ads

  • Interstitial ads: Full-screen ads that may appear between levels (not on every level, and never during the first few levels of gameplay)
  • Rewarded ads: Optional video ads that the user can choose to watch in exchange for in-game benefits (such as extra lives). These are never forced.

5.2 AdMob and Data

Google AdMob may collect certain information to serve and personalize ads, including device identifiers, IP address, and general usage data. This data collection is governed by Google's own privacy policy, not ours. We do not have access to or control over data collected by AdMob.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob & Privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

5.3 Ad Personalization & Opt-Out

You may opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device's ad settings:

  • Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads > "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy > Apple Advertising > Turn off "Personalized Ads"

5A. Usage Analytics and Crash Reports

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is actually played, so that we can fix what is broken — for example which level people get stuck on, or how often a player taps a piece that cannot move. It also uses Firebase Crashlytics so that crashes are reported to us automatically.

What is recorded

  • Gameplay events: level started, completed or failed, with the level number, the difficulty tier, stars earned, number of wrong taps and how long the level took
  • Hint usage, taps on blocked pieces, levels left unfinished
  • Daily goal completion and day-streak length
  • Which screens are opened, and when an advertisement is shown
  • Crash and error reports, including the device state at the time of the crash
  • Standard technical information collected automatically by Firebase: an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language and country (derived from IP address; the IP address itself is not retained by Firebase for Analytics)

What is not recorded

  • Nothing that identifies you personally — no name, email or contact details
  • No contact list, no message content, no precise location

These events are aggregated: we look at totals and averages across all players and do not use them to identify an individual. Data is processed by Google LLC as described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation, and is retained for up to 14 months.

5B. Cloud Backup of Game Progress

So that your progress is not lost when you change or reset your device, the App can back it up to Google Cloud Firestore.

  • What is stored: your game progress only — stars earned per level, highest level reached, current level, day-streak and daily goal count. No personal information and no contact details.
  • How it is identified: the App signs in anonymously to Firebase Authentication, which produces a random identifier not linked to your identity. If you choose “Back up with Google” in the settings, that identifier is linked to your Google account so the backup can be restored on another device. This is optional; the App works fully without it.
  • Who can read it: only you. Security rules restrict each saved record so that it can be read and written solely by the account that created it.
  • Device settings are not synced: sound, music, vibration, language and voice preferences stay on the device.

To have your cloud backup deleted, see Section 11.

6. Text-to-Speech Feature

The App uses your device's built-in text-to-speech (TTS) engine to read aloud letters, numbers, and shapes during gameplay. This feature operates entirely on your device using the operating system's native TTS capabilities. No audio data, voice recordings, or speech data is collected, stored, or transmitted.

7. General Audience

Worm Out! is designed as a general audience puzzle game and is not directed at children. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from any user, including minors. The App contains no social features, no chat and no user-generated content. We do not build advertising or behavioural profiles of individual players: the usage analytics described in Section 5A are aggregated, and advertising is handled by Google AdMob under the settings described in Section 5.

10. Data Security

We do not collect names, email addresses or other directly identifying information, so there is no personal profile of you to be exposed. The analytics, advertising and backup data described in Sections 5, 5A and 5B is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored by Google under its own security controls; cloud backups are further restricted by security rules so that only the account that created a record can read it. Game progress and preferences held on your device are protected by the standard security measures of your device's operating system. We recommend keeping your operating system up to date to benefit from the latest security patches.

11. Data Retention & Deletion

Game progress and preferences held on your device can be deleted at any time by:

  • Clearing the App's data/cache through your device's application settings
  • Uninstalling the App from your device

Upon deletion, all locally stored game progress and preferences are permanently removed.

Cloud backup

A cloud backup (Section 5B) is not removed by uninstalling the App. To have it deleted, email muhammetozata1991@gmail.com from the Google account linked to the backup, or — if the backup was never linked to a Google account — state that you would like your anonymous backup deleted and we will remove it. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

Analytics and advertising

Analytics events (Section 5A) are retained by Google Analytics for Firebase for up to 14 months and are then deleted automatically. Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies; you can reset or restrict your advertising identifier from your device settings (Section 5.3).
